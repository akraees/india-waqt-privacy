India Waqt privacy policy

Effective 21 November 2026 · App: India Waqt (in.raees.prayertimes)

In short: India Waqt is free and has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts. We do not collect, store or sell your personal data. Your location and settings stay on your phone.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your phone's location to calculate prayer times, the Qibla direction and sunrise and sunset times. These calculations run on your phone. India Waqt does not send your location to us or to any other company. You can choose a city instead of using location, and you can turn location permission off at any time in your phone's settings.

The mosque finder opens a search in Google Maps. Once Google Maps is open, Google's own privacy policy applies to what you do there.

Information stored on your phone

Your name for greetings (optional), chosen city, prayer settings, reminders, bookmarks, prayer and fasting logs, and similar preferences are saved only on your phone. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. If you use the app's backup feature, the backup file is saved where you choose, and you control it.

Downloads from the internet

Some content is downloaded only when you ask for it. This includes Quran translations and recitations, and hadith translation packs. To deliver these files, the servers that host them, such as GitHub, jsDelivr and MP3Quran, receive a standard web request from your phone, which includes your IP address. India Waqt does not send them any personal information, and we do not receive any data from these requests.

Notifications and alarms

Prayer alerts, the azan and reminders are scheduled on your phone. They use Android's notification, exact alarm and full-screen alarm permissions only to alert you at the times you choose.

Sharing

When you share an ayah, hadith, dua or prayer times, the app creates the image or text on your phone and passes it to the app you pick. Nothing is sent to us.

Children

India Waqt is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
