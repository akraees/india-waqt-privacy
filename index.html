India Waqt privacy policy

Effective 26 November 2026 (version 1.1) · App: India Waqt (in.raees.prayertimes)

In short: India Waqt is free to use and has no accounts. Your location, settings and logs stay on your phone, and we do not collect, store or sell your personal data. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below. India Waqt Pro removes ads and is bought through Google Play.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your phone's location to calculate prayer times, the Qibla direction and sunrise and sunset times. These calculations run on your phone. India Waqt does not send your location to us or to any other company. You can choose a city instead of using location, and you can turn location permission off at any time in your phone's settings.

The mosque finder opens a search in Google Maps. Once Google Maps is open, Google's own privacy policy applies to what you do there.

Information stored on your phone

Your name for greetings (optional), chosen city, prayer settings, reminders, bookmarks, prayer and fasting logs, and similar preferences are saved only on your phone. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. If you use the app's backup feature, the backup file is saved where you choose, and you control it.

Downloads from the internet

Some content is downloaded only when you ask for it. This includes Quran translations and recitations, and hadith translation packs. To deliver these files, the servers that host them, such as GitHub, jsDelivr and MP3Quran, receive a standard web request from your phone, which includes your IP address. India Waqt does not send them any personal information, and we do not receive any data from these requests.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version of India Waqt shows a banner ad from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. India Waqt itself does not collect data for advertising and does not share your location, name, settings or logs with Google or anyone else. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID / app set ID),
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location may be inferred,
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version,
  • ad interactions such as views and taps, and diagnostic data such as crash logs of the ads code.

Google uses this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. The data is sent encrypted.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time under Settings › About & credits › Ad privacy choices. Without consent, you may see limited or no ads. On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Google › Ads). Ads stop entirely when you have India Waqt Pro.

India Waqt Pro and payments

Pro is sold as a subscription (monthly or yearly, renewing until you cancel) or a one-time purchase, all through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details, name, address or email. The app receives only what Google Play Billing reports to every app: which product was bought, the purchase state and a purchase token. The app uses this on your phone to turn Pro on, to keep it on across reinstalls on the same Google account, and to turn it off again if a subscription ends or is refunded. You can cancel a subscription any time in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions. Google's handling of the purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

The 7-day Pro trial and any Pro days earned in the app are recorded only on your phone.

Notifications and alarms

Prayer alerts, the azan and reminders are scheduled on your phone. They use Android's notification, exact alarm and full-screen alarm permissions only to alert you at the times you choose.

Sharing

When you share an ayah, hadith, dua or prayer times, the app creates the image or text on your phone and passes it to the app you pick. Nothing is sent to us.

Children

India Waqt is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app has no sign-up or account. If you believe a child's data was collected through the ads in the app, contact us and we will help you ask Google to delete it.

Data retention and deletion

Everything the app stores is on your phone and is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. We keep no copy. Data collected by Google for ads or purchases is kept and deleted under Google's policies; you can review and delete activity linked to your Google account at myaccount.google.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
